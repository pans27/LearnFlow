Team 24 developed LearnFlow, a learning platform that connects instructors and students through shared course workspaces. Instructors organize materials, publish announcements, create assessments, and grade submissions, while students access resources, complete coursework, and view results. Administrators support these activities by managing users, courses, and enrollment. These workflows are powered by a React and TypeScript interface connected to Express APIs, with PostgreSQL storing course records and AWS S3 managing uploaded files. Role-based access control ensures appropriate permissions. We are also developing PDF text extraction and full-text search to help users find information within course documents.
